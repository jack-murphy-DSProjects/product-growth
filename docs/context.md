# Context Vocabulary

Use these terms consistently across docs and future code.

- Account: A B2B customer account.
- User: A person associated with an account.
- Subscription: The commercial plan and contract state for an account.
- Usage event: A product interaction generated by a user.
- Support ticket: A support interaction tied to an account or user.
- CRM touchpoint: A sales, success, or lifecycle interaction.
- Renewal: A contract renewal event or renewal opportunity.
- Account-month: A point-in-time monthly snapshot for one account.
- Churn risk: The probability or risk band for near-term churn.
- Expansion propensity: The probability or likelihood of account expansion.
- Health band: A human-readable account health category.
- Recommendation: A GTM action suggested by scores and business rules.
- Generated artefact: Any local output created by pipelines, training,
  scoring, monitoring, notebooks, or model tooling.
